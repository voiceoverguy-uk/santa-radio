General enquiries and song submissions use one shared form. General enquiry is the default; song entry links preselect Song submission.

**Why:** The owner supplied a new Song Suggestion Form action for song submissions only. This supersedes the earlier approval to share the old destination; General enquiry must keep its existing destination and behaviour.

**How to apply:** Preserve separate provider destinations as well as mode-specific guidance and confirmation. Do not add provider attributes, lists or automations to separate routing, or reroute the independent Santa-message signup flow.

Use the current read-only hosted song form and the newest owner-supplied embed to confirm the provider field contract. Older embeds may be historical evidence; a visible placeholder is not a provider attribute name. Report disagreements rather than guessing field mappings.

**Why:** The hosted message contract changed after the original supplied embed. The owner authorised alignment with the confirmed live field, but excluded Brevo attribute, list and automation changes. Read-only form inspection and mocked acceptance do not establish actual record persistence.

**How to apply:** Recheck the hosted contract when maintaining song submission and report unexpected differences. Do not attribute missing surname personalization or claim persisted messages based on outgoing requests. A live persistence or automatic-email check needs separate owner approval.

Song submissions must not collect a phone number or country code. Their message criteria include a link to any promotion of Santa Radio, alongside the artist, song, listening link and short bio or description.

**Why:** The owner requested removal of the phone line and addition of the promotion-link criterion.

**How to apply:** Preserve these requirements in future form changes, including shared contact/song forms. The “Have you met the criteria?” checkbox confirms the submission requirements; it is not marketing consent or a new Brevo contact attribute.
