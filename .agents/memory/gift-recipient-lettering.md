description: Owner-approved legible recipient names, separate from Santa's decorative signature.
---
Use Lora Italic for recipient names on Nice List certificates and Santa's parchment pictures. Keep Santa's signature decorative and preserve the approved artwork and letter body typography.

**Why:** The owner approved this recommendation because Great Vibes' ornate capital A was difficult for children to recognise.

**How to apply:** Keep compact and enlarged previews consistent with PDFs and PNGs. Include “Big Dog Alice”, “Alice”, “Arabella” and “Ayla” in visual checks. Preserve exact copied PDF spelling as well as appearance; canonically equivalent accented spellings can look identical while having different Unicode text.
