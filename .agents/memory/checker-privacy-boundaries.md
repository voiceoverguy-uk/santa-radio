selection, Studio access, purchases and personalised-message state. Do not extend
it into behavioural tracking, saved results, public lists, sharing
exports or child-name analytics without new owner direction.

and required names and answers to stay in memory, never submitted or retained.

**How to apply:** Preserve local display-name-only suggestions; an absent name
must never imply Naughty. Cross-page child-name handoffs need actual ephemeral
memory, not browser history state: browsers can serialise history state to disk.

for Nice and Naughty, both with the entered name, an enlarged preview and a
browser-local PDF download. Stamp the Naughty version with the red
words “THERE IS STILL TIME”. No server archive or persisted play state.

**Why:** The owner asked for the message maker's named Nice certificate, then
requested a matching Naughty version with a red encouraging stamp. They later
requested separate Head Elf wording/signatures and downloads for both outcomes,
superseding the earlier Nice view-only restriction.

**How to apply:** Reuse the approved artwork and recipient lettering locally,
without changing the Studio's masters, purchase or gift-delivery boundaries.
