---
name: Studio audio retention
description: Generated audio and keepsakes are temporary for paid and voucher visits; emailed links begin creation, never recover files.
---

For paid and free/voucher visits, finished Studio MP3s exist only during preparation and in the open browser page.
Keep the temporary recording available for playback and download retries during the visit,
then clear it when the session ends or expires. Delete temporary server working files after
each request, including failures and cancellation. Do not store generated audio,
choice history or download history. The later two-creation website offer
permits minimal success counts per verified purchase and expiring counts for
the owner-selected limited voucher's authorised visitor session only.

**Why:** The owner explicitly simplified Studio to generate, play and save,
superseding the earlier durable generation/correction and recovery rules.

**Why:** The later owner-approved two-creations flow supersedes the earlier
ban on minimal allowance accounting, but not output retention.

**How to apply:** Keep source-recording caches separate from generated output:
Mark's original source library is not a generated-message archive. Preserve
existing records without reading them as a prerequisite for new creation.
Keep real voucher/payment access checks and existing expiring abuse buckets;
do not repurpose them into a hidden generation-history store. Never make
unlimited-voucher creation depend on generation/reporting tables. Paid and
limited-voucher allowance storage must fail closed if unavailable.

The website may retain two completed recordings and their matching immutable
gift details during the open visit, including while the second is being edited.
Beginning the second is non-destructive; unlimited vouchers must not turn this
into an unbounded recording history.

**Why:** The owner authorised keeping both website Studio messages locally,
superseding the single-result discard assumption, not the no-archive policy.

**How to apply:** Preserve both through exhausted allowance and temporary
access-check errors. Clear both on refresh, exit or real access expiry. An
explicitly confirmed destructive reset clears every retained output. Keep the
first completed gifts retrievable during second-message composition and after
failure, separately from the unfinished draft’s locked gift previews.

Present completed website recordings newest-first, but keep First/Second labels
and gift selectors tied to chronological creation identity.

**Why:** The owner superseded creation-order presentation only, not recording
identity, gift associations or the two-creation allowance.

**How to apply:** Reordering cards must not reorder the underlying completions,
redirect READY away from the latest recording, or replenish limited access.

Download All is a per-recording ZIP, not a ZIP combining both creations. It
contains that completion's original MP3 and three gifts, independent of the
selected gift preview or unfinished edits. Keep individual downloads available.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose one ZIP per finished recording to avoid
browsers blocking four automatic downloads from a single click.

**How to apply:** Preserve the completion's written name and letter wording;
never reconstruct a bundle from current editable choices. Preparation and
retries remain local and use no additional creation allowance.

Download reminders should pulse independently for each recording until its
audio or bundle download is requested. Downloading one must leave the other's
reminder glowing; reduced motion gets a steady glow.

**Why:** The owner wants to remind visitors to keep both temporary recordings,
not stop all reminders after downloading just one.

**How to apply:** Track each completion separately during the visit. The browser
cannot prove a file was saved, so do not use these flags as server-side delivery
proof or a reason to discard the recordings.

Show the owner's chosen word “Downloaded” directly under each clicked Audio
or Download All button, independently across all four buttons. Do not show
the former long ZIP-request disclaimer after a successful request.
After Download All, the corresponding three individual gift feedback lines
also say “Downloaded”; individually downloaded gifts use the same wording.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested simpler, button-specific feedback.

**How to apply:** Mark only the button whose download was requested; retain
real preparation/error feedback, and do not mark failed ZIP preparation as a
new successful download.

Paid website purchases are not an exception to temporary audio handling.
Do not permanently store generated audio or keepsakes or build a recording-retrieval service.
The emailed link gives a verified paying customer access to BEGIN creating a message,
not access to saved audio or previous downloads.

**Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the conflicting project notes and confirmed
that customers create, play and download their message during that visit.

**How to apply:** Preserve voucher access independently. Keep the temporary browser recording
through playback and download retries; release it at the end of the visit or access expiry.
Payment authorisation records are not permission to archive generated outputs.
