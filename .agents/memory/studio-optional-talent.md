Talent is one optional recording overall, either Sports & activities or Something
funny, immediately after Age. Changing category discards the line and search;
skipping must be deliberate. This supersedes the earlier required sport-only
search page.

**Why:** The owner revised the scope to include funny recordings and avoid
requiring a talent. No automatic selections or silently restored category lines
are wanted.

**How to apply:** Preserve exact owner filenames and the sibling folders `sports`
and `funny` under `personalised-message`. Shared `1-5.wav` belongs in that root.
Visible step renumbering never renames existing connector files. Omit both
shared introduction and talent audio on skip; never add talent prose to the
approved letter/certificate or change the free maker, pricing or access.

External uploads remain the owner's action. Direct HTTP success for a recording
does not establish index discovery, decoding, listening or exact wording.

**Why:** The owner explicitly excluded external-host modification and speech
creation/editing. The original hosted index did not discover the new groups.
Automated transcription of the shared connector suggested minor differences
from approved display wording.

**How to apply:** Follow `docs/workshop-https-inventory.md` for upload and connector
evidence, but recheck current hosting rather than treating historical status as
permanent. A replacement hosted connector also needs a refreshed packaged
lossless source; changing helper text cannot change speech. Never insert test
fixture lines into the live builder to conceal unavailable discovery.
