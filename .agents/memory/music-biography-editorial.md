name: Christmas Music biography editorial policy
description: Voice, sourcing and identity rules for Christmas Music biographies, separate from Mugshots.
---
For Christmas Music artist/song biographies, use concise original British English, normally 45–70 words, with recognisable career highlights and a verified seasonal recording or featured song. The Santa Radio voice should be warm and personable, with occasional gentle family-friendly humour, not a joke in every paragraph.

Avoid generic praise, repetitive festive sparkle, gossip, inflated superlatives, current ages, changing award totals, fabricated quotations or claims that artists endorse or listen to Santa Radio.

**Why:** The user requested “Doesn’t need to be big, but maybe fun too??” and explicitly separated this work from Mugshots.

**How to apply:** Match existing pages and recordings before writing. Use official artist/label sites or reputable music references, retaining sources and before/after copy in an internal editorial record. Describe joint performances accurately, not as permanent groups. Leave ambiguous attribution unchanged and report the specific question. Preserve page addresses, artwork, songs, lyrics and players.
