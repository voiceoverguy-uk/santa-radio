Santa Dash has a little character running on rooftops. Christmas town backgrounds should show houses and chimneys from the side, with the town set back so houses are visible in the distance behind the playable foreground. New areas must match the distant town scale of the original three scenes, even if the buildings, people and other details become small.

**Why:** The user explained the gameplay and supplied phone screenshots to distinguish the background town from the rooftop platforms. They rejected the first extensions as “really close up” beside the “beautiful distant scenery of the original 3” and explicitly said they do not mind the images being small.

**How to apply:** Use the original three distant villages as the scale reference, not the earlier close-up extensions. Keep towns low and small in the landscape, with abundant sky and mountains; do not enlarge landmarks, people, reindeer or the UFO for readability. Avoid baking the character, interface or close playable platforms into the image. The requested panorama export size is 4096 × 1242 PNG.

Deliver Santa Dash game backgrounds as PNG, not WebP.

**Why:** The owner clarified: “the app only does png or jpg” and asked to go back to PNG files.

**How to apply:** Make the game-ready set PNG-only, with individually downloadable PNGs and a clearly labelled PNG-only archive. Keep WebP out of app-facing artwork packages.

Multi-image scrolling scenery must also loop from the far right of the final image back to the left of the first image.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that the far right must match the left-hand side of image 1.

**How to apply:** Check every internal join and the outer wrap; adjacent tiles should come from one continuous panorama, not unrelated images.

Keep the illustrated snowy alpine style of the approved woodland-blended looping panorama when extending it into additional scenes.

**Why:** The owner called the first continuous panorama “amazing” and asked to join three more scenes onto it.

**How to apply:** Extend the journey rather than replacing the approved original scenery; daylight and more imaginative Christmas locations may follow it, with the final scene returning to the original first image.

Keep the Santa Dash end-screen labels horizontally anchored to their original positions and align their vertical optical centres to the in-game scores.

**Why:** The user explained that the score appears to the right of the labels and explicitly requested keeping their positions. Their in-game screenshot later showed that matching old italic text bounds made the new lettering sit too low, especially TOTAL.

**How to apply:** Preserve horizontal placement and leave score areas free of added labels or decorative elements. For vertical alignment, trust the actual game screenshot over the original artwork's ink bounds; different fonts can require a small optical lift.

Santa Dash's Treasures page is to be a large Santa Radio advert with an App Store download button. The owner will link the button in Xcode wherever it is placed in the image.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this replacement for the existing Treasures screen.

**How to apply:** Deliver a static image and precise button bounds in source-image coordinates, with the App Store destination. Do not claim that a PNG itself contains a clickable link or implement game navigation without the game project.
