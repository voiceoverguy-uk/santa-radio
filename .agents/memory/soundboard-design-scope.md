The owner approved the workshop concept for the homepage soundboard: forest green, warm fairy lights, pine and red baubles at the edges, gold framing, ruby playing state and an integrated now-playing strip. They subsequently requested removal of Santa’s face/portrait from the surround; keep the workshop scenery and vintage microphone without restoring the portrait.

**Why:** The owner supplied and approved the visual concept and explicitly requested a usable soundboard rather than a flat poster, then explicitly asked to remove Santa’s face from the surround.

**How to apply:** Preserve all 20 existing phrase labels, their order and audio recordings. Keep the same-phrase stop / different-phrase replacement interaction. Show playing visuals only after playback begins, not merely after selection. Keep artwork secondary to readable controls. Soundboard changes must remain separate from ongoing Apps-page work.
