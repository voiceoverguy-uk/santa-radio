---
name: Apps ratings eligibility
description: Why verified third-party store ratings are not app rich-result candidates on this site.
---
Keep store-information panels off the Apps page, without adding replacement verification messages. Retain the apps and their store download buttons.

**Why:** The owner wants a simpler page without repeated store names, developers, prices, ratings, minimum iOS versions, sources or check dates.

**How to apply:** Direct visitors to the linked stores for current pricing. Preserve screenshot provenance, Amazon device guidance and the separate device showcases.

Public store ratings can be displayed with clear store/region attribution and a check date, but must not be republished as Santa Radio aggregate-rating markup.

**Why:** Google's software-app guidance explicitly requires compliance with review snippet guidelines, which prohibit aggregating ratings from other websites. Verified store identity and price alone do not make a rating eligible.

**How to apply:** Keep catalogue-only markup unless genuinely eligible first-party review evidence is available. Recheck the current Google rules before changing this decision; do not treat a store snapshot as the site's own review collection.
