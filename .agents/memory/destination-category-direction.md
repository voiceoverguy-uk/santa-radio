Fresh destination entry starts with “Countries & real places”, not a category inferred from wheel position or search. Keep “Fantasy & space” as a deliberate separate choice; editing an existing destination must expose it in its matching category.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the countries default while separating the mixed destination wheel, without moving or renaming recordings.

**How to apply:** Preserve the verified catalogue classifications and existing recording identities during future destination layout changes. State/county selection stays optional and its action belongs directly beneath the selected-country wheel label, not inside explanatory copy.

Centre the “Choose a county/state” action against the full country-wheel box, not the remaining space beside the selected-country text, consistently across all eligible countries.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested moving these actions left into the middle of the box.

**How to apply:** Keep the selected-country text and optional region behaviour; allow a separate centred action row on narrow screens so the text and link do not overlap.

Keep “Back to countries” as an unboxed text action in all five regional selectors.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested removing the box around that control for all five options.

**How to apply:** Preserve its navigation, keyboard focus and touch target; do not restore a visible border or filled button on hover.

Do not append “— Fantasy place” or “— Space destination” to displayed destination names, including the wheel, Santa's wording preview and review. The Moon should display as “The Moon”.

**Why:** The owner said the clickable category option at the top already identifies the section, and explicitly requested removing “— Space destination” from The Moon.

**How to apply:** Keep the category control and recording identities unchanged; remove the redundant suffix only from presentation.
