Fresh destination entry starts with “Countries & real places”, not a category inferred from wheel position or search. Keep “Fantasy & space” as a deliberate separate choice; editing an existing destination must expose it in its matching category.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the countries default while separating the mixed destination wheel, without moving or renaming recordings.

**How to apply:** Preserve the verified catalogue classifications and existing recording identities during future destination layout changes. State/county selection stays optional and its action belongs directly beneath the selected-country wheel label, not inside explanatory copy.

Do not append “— Fantasy place” to displayed destination names, including the wheel, Santa's wording preview and review.

**Why:** The owner said the clickable category option at the top already identifies the section.

**How to apply:** Keep the category control and recording identities unchanged; remove the redundant suffix only from presentation.
