---
name: Destination category direction
description: Owner's default destination category and separation from recording storage.
---

Fresh destination entry starts with “Countries & real places”, not a category inferred from wheel position or search. Keep “Fantasy & space” as a deliberate separate choice; editing an existing destination must expose it in its matching category.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the countries default while separating the mixed destination wheel, without moving or renaming recordings.

**How to apply:** Preserve the verified catalogue classifications and existing recording identities during future destination layout changes. State/county selection stays optional and its action belongs directly beneath the selected-country wheel label, not inside explanatory copy.

On desktop, centre the “Choose a county/state” action against the full country-wheel box, not the remaining space beside the selected-country text. On mobile, place it at the bottom-right of the selector footer.

**Why:** The owner originally requested centred actions, then explicitly changed mobile placement to the bottom-right of the box.

**How to apply:** Keep selected-country text and optional region behaviour; use a separate right-aligned action row on mobile so text and links do not overlap. Preserve desktop centring.

On mobile, after a county/state is highlighted, place “Use [country] only” at the bottom-right of the regional selector footer, below the selected-region text.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested this position once a county is highlighted.

**How to apply:** Preserve the action's existing region-clearing behaviour and selected country, and leave desktop placement unchanged.

Do not show “Back to countries” in any of the five regional selectors. The active “Countries & real places” category button is the way back.

**Why:** The owner subsequently requested removing the control itself because users can click “Countries & real places”.

**How to apply:** Preserve category-button navigation, focus and selected regions. Keep the distinct “Use [country] only” action available after selecting a region.

Do not append “— Fantasy place” or “— Space destination” to displayed destination names, including the wheel, Santa's wording preview and review. The Moon should display as “The Moon”.

**Why:** The owner said the clickable category option at the top already identifies the section, and explicitly requested removing “— Space destination” from The Moon.

**How to apply:** Keep the category control and recording identities unchanged; remove the redundant suffix only from presentation.

The owner's additional Fantasy destinations include Planet Brussels Sprout, The Land of Missing Socks, Behind the Sofa, Under the Bed, The Temple of Doom, The Lost City of Gold, Treasure Island, Shangri-La, Laputa, Candy Kingdom, Green Hill Zone, The Mushroom Kingdom, Tatooine, A galaxy far, far away, Metropolis, Gotham City, Hogsmeade, Hogwarts, Bikini Bottom and The Biscuit Tin. Arendelle is excluded from this addition.

**Why:** The owner supplied the proposed recordings and list, asking that all of these except Arendelle go into Fantasy.

**How to apply:** Classify recordings when they appear in the hosted inventory; preserve their actual filenames and IDs, including capitalised names with spaces. Do not fabricate selectable recordings before upload.
