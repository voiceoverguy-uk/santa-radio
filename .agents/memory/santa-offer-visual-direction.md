name: Santa message offer visual direction
description: Owner-corrected popup size, backdrop, shimmer and holly expectations.
---

Keep the Santa message offer centred and full length rather than restricted to 75% height, with about 30% backdrop opacity so the website remains recognisable. Remove the top ornament and lift the text slightly to reduce height.

**Why:** After trying the 75% version, the owner explicitly rejected the scrollable result and requested full length again with less decoration and top spacing.

**How to apply:** Fit the whole form on ordinary desktop screens without an internal scrolling panel. Retain a safety overflow path only where necessary to avoid cutting off controls on short screens or with a keyboard/enlarged text. Keep the lighter backdrop.

Make the word FREE itself shimmer/sparkle. Do not add decorative stars or glints on either side of it. The unlock button may retain its animated shimmer/glints. Holly should have recognisable pointed/serrated leaves and red berries.

**Why:** The owner repeated the distinction between making the word sparkle and adding extra things beside it, and supplied a screenshot marking the unwanted side stars.

**How to apply:** Keep animation gentle and purely decorative, respect reduced-motion preferences, and preserve readable form text and focus controls.

Allow the mouse wheel outside the open offer to scroll the website behind it, without closing the offer. Inside-panel wheel gestures belong to the form, and background controls should remain inert.

**Why:** The owner requested scrolling the website behind the pop-up when clicking away from it.

**How to apply:** Preserve modal focus containment and form details while permitting outside-panel wheel scrolling; do not make background buttons or links interactive.
