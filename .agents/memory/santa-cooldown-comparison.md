The “Coming soon, you can personalise your message more for the price of…” comparison must stay the same while the page is open. A page refresh may choose another gift.

**Why:** The owner said this does not need to keep changing and can change on a page refresh.

**How to apply:** Do not add timed rotation or reselect when the cooldown countdown updates. Keep the comparison stable through client-side navigation too.

The free-message availability notice should say “Your next free message available at (time).” Show only the visitor's local clock time, without the weekday or date.

**Why:** The owner requested this shorter wording from a screenshot of the two-hour wait notice.

**How to apply:** Keep the two-hour allowance and stored deadline unchanged; use the same short wording in the completed-recording and waiting states.
