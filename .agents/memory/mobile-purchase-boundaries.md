---
name: Native purchase and session boundaries
description: Why mobile paid usage must remain separate from the website and temporary recording delivery.
---
The owner approved separate iPhone products: £24.99 permanent Studio access and
£4.99 per completed generation. These do not replace or unlock the website's
£6.50 family bundle. The initial native build is sandbox-only; connecting
RevenueCat is not permission to activate live Apple commerce.

**Why:** The native app is a separate product with Apple purchase requirements,
while the website's working checkout must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Keep website cookies, grants and purchase accounting isolated
from native bearer authentication. RevenueCat customer identities must be
server-generated. Verify purchases on the server, and deduplicate consumables
by the underlying Apple transaction across anonymous identity transfers.

Successful generation usage may be retained for replay prevention, but MP3s
must not become a permanent delivery/recovery archive.

**Why:** The owner requested temporary audio handling and no new user accounts;
expiring successful purchase usage would nevertheless permit restore replay.

**How to apply:** Retain only minimal usage identifiers. Release failed render
reservations. Do not add email-based purchase recovery or permanent recordings
without explicit approval.
