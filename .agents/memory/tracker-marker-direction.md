description: Owner's geographic interpretation of GMT and seasonal map artwork boundaries.
---
For seasonal Santa artwork, “GMT” means the geographic Greenwich meridian at longitude zero, not a destination's time zone. Holiday/scout side views face right west of Greenwich, mirror east of Greenwich, and use matching front views at zero. Sleigh artwork faces left normally and right only on actual eastward backtracking legs.

**Why:** The owner explicitly specified geographic direction, preserving the existing annual seasons and itinerary rather than deriving orientation from local clocks or map movement.

**How to apply:** Keep full website and homepage maps consistent. Do not extend this approval to native iPhone artwork, other Santa emoji, or preparation/completion trip labels.
