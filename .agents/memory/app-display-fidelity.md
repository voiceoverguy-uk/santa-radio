---
name: App display fidelity
description: Approval boundary for replacing app promotional compositions with physical device displays.
---

Keep the identity and interface shown in approved examples. Do not silently substitute visibly older standalone screenshots, generate replacement interface artwork, or put complete posters inside new phones.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires genuine app displays inside convincing physical phones while preserving the approved app examples, not merely a matching app name.

**How to apply:** Perspective correction is appropriate only where the source contains a sufficiently unobscured display. Foreground signs, fingers or speech bubbles are not recoverable by perspective correction. When a supplied composition contains no phone display at all, request current screen-only artwork or explicit approval of the available older gameplay capture. Preserve promotional originals.

The owner approved “Use existing screen-only images” for Santa Voicemail, Santa Messages, Santa Text and Santa Dash.

**Why:** Their promotional compositions did not provide clean, recoverable full displays; Dash's approved compositions were illustrations rather than gameplay.

**How to apply:** These four existing screen-only originals are authorised for phone presentation. This is not permission to replace Santa Radio's current four screens or Christmas Radio's newer approved interface with older screenshots.

Santa Text's older screen-only approval has been superseded by the owner's four
native captures: welcome, signpost menu, Santa Dash promotion and Santa chat.
The website-reference capture identifies the section and is not app artwork.

**Why:** The owner requested the current native app appearance while keeping the
existing dark portrait handset, section copy and download destination.

**How to apply:** Preserve each full composition and its native proportions.
Do not restore the retired display when preparing assets, generate replacement
UI, or add a second camera cut-out over the chat capture's own.

The owner subsequently approved the supplied “Santa Dash — Back to the Grotto”
rooftop promotional artwork inside a dark landscape phone on `/apps`, replacing
the earlier gameplay approval for this section only.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the new artwork and a handset matching
Santa Text. This is an exception to the general screen-only fidelity rule.

**How to apply:** Preserve the full title, Santa scene and bottom caption. Do not
extend this exception to other app displays, the game or the homepage advert.

A Safari broken-image screenshot does not establish that artwork is missing or corrupt. Keep recovery measures separate from claims about the original cause.

**Why:** Reported broken displays have coexisted with intact originals, successful preview delivery and successful Chromium decoding. A compatible fallback can reduce failures without proving which Safari request or decoder failed.

**How to apply:** Preserve approved originals and verify both response bytes and browser decoding. Describe fallback recovery as recovery, not as proof of a Safari fix, until the affected Safari session can be checked.
