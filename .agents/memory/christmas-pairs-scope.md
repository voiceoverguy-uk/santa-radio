Christmas Pairs is a free website game below the homepage soundboard, inspired by the owner's existing app. It must not require signup. Use the owner's supplied card artwork rather than inventing replacements.

Use the owner's supplied square Santa Radio mascot logo, small and centred, on
the card backs instead of the star and “SR”.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the logo replacement and supplied its image.

**How to apply:** Keep the logo's proportions, green card backs and gold framing;
do not change the matching pictures or game behaviour.

**Why:** The owner requested the app-style pairs game on the website and supplied the picture collection.

**How to apply:** Preserve the remember-then-match flow, increasing card counts, restart and pause controls. The owner authorised choosing sensible preview timings; screenshots are visual references, not evidence of exact app timing or unseen level rules.

The owner authorised generating a temporary workshop background for the game while continuing to supply the card pictures themselves.

**Why:** The owner said “Can you create a background for now and I'll add more pics shortly.”

**How to apply:** The supplied-artwork restriction applies to matching-card faces, not to the temporary decorative background.

The outer section should resemble subtle dark-green festive wrapping paper, not an extension of the workshop scenery. Keep photographic branches, baubles and lights out of that surrounding section; retain the workshop backdrop inside the game.

**Why:** The owner requested restrained wrapping paper and clarified that the reference identifies the surrounding section, not the workshop scene inside the playable panel.

**How to apply:** Keep the outer pattern small, faint, static and separate from game content. Preserve the supplied card pictures, title, scores, controls and gameplay when changing that backdrop.

Sound effects are optional and off initially; keep them separate from radio, soundboard and visual-effects preferences.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose “Optional sound, off initially.”

**How to apply:** Only enable game audio after deliberate interaction with its own sound control; do not mute or interrupt other website audio as a side effect.
