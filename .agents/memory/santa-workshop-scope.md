name: Santa workshop preview scope
description: Owner approval boundary between the extended-message design trial and future audio work
---

The owner has also approved on-demand playback of the selected existing recorded name in the final review, so visitors can check its pronunciation. This does not approve creating or playing a full extended message.

**Why:** They requested a small red play button beside the review name: “That way they can make sure it’s correct.”

**How to apply:** Play the exact chosen hosted recording, including its variant, only after a click. Do not derive its audio filename from a typed or certificate spelling. Stop when editing or leaving the review and coordinate with the radio without overriding manual radio choices. Name-only playback must not consume a message allowance or submit typed child details.

The owner chose to try an isolated design-first extended-message workshop before connecting MP3 creation. The offline extended-message proof establishes selection categories, not an approved catalogue or audio service.

**Why:** The owner wants to review the page experience first without replacing or changing the existing free maker.

**How to apply:** Keep the trial separate from the existing free maker. The owner now wants it introduced publicly on the homepage with a voucher-code option while payment options are undecided. Do not interpret promotion or design approval as approval to connect full-message recordings or change the free maker’s signup and message permissions.

The first workshop stage must use the actual FTP-hosted name list, not a fixed Arabella example.

**Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the sample-only name picker: “This first box should be pulling from the list on the FTP.” Arabella came from an offline proof and is not the workshop's fixed name.

**How to apply:** Load hosted inventories while keeping extended-message audio disconnected. Filter typed names locally so child details do not enter request URLs or server logs. The owner subsequently requested actual hosted choices for ages, activities, achievements, treats and destinations too; offline examples must not substitute for those lists. This is permission to display the inventory, not approval of every recording’s wording, playback or future mixing.

The owner has recorded ages 1–50. The age stage must offer that full range in a compact vertical scroll wheel, not paged cards, number blocks, or the two offline sample ages.

**Why:** After rejecting paged age cards, the owner clarified: “I keep mentionaing a carousel style scroll wheel” and supplied an iOS-style vertical picker reference.

**How to apply:** Use a centred vertical wheel with touch, mouse scrolling and keyboard selection. Do not silently preselect an initial age. The recorded range is confirmed by the owner; this does not connect or verify remote audio playback.

The name choice should retain the homepage/free maker’s familiar cream search input and matching autocomplete dropdown, not a green grid of name cards.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested: “Keep the familiarity as the homepage for searching please for the Name choice.”

**How to apply:** Preserve hosted-list lookup and recording variants while presenting matches as a compact dropdown beneath the input.

The workshop should support sound-alike recorded names with a separate final written-name spelling for the certificate and mixed-message filename.

**Why:** The owner asked for a no-match hint suggesting another spelling that sounds the same, and an end-of-flow spelling change reflected in the filename and certificate.

**How to apply:** Keep recording identity and spoken name unchanged. Treat written spelling as separate in-memory input unless the visitor explicitly sends the optional suggestion described below; show its intended output uses honestly in the design preview and apply it to real outputs when those are connected. Do not claim that changing letters changes Santa’s pronunciation.

Written-name suggestions must be opt-in, separate from using the corrected spelling.

**Why:** The owner chose “Let visitors choose to send it” rather than automatic saving on spelling confirmation. The owner wants a private list pairing requested spellings with the exact selected recording, to review for later recording or manual duplication.

**How to apply:** Only the separate send action may persist the requested first-name spelling and hosted-recording identity with counts/dates. Never save draft searches, other message choices or contact details, and do not rename or duplicate FTP files automatically.

Use HTTPS for the hosted workshop catalogue, like the working names inventory, rather than requiring an FTP login.

**Why:** The owner reiterated that the recordings are inside the existing names folder and corrected the FTP detour: “I thought you were accessing it with HTTPS now??”

**How to apply:** Distinguish reading a known public recording from discovering filenames. A read-only HTTPS folder index can supply the complete catalogue; a failed FTP certificate check does not imply that HTTPS audio access is broken. Preserve the existing names index and recordings.

Short interface sound effects are allowed in the design preview even though personalised-message audio creation is not connected.

**Why:** The owner supplied a wheel-click MP3 and separately asked for their message-next MP3 to play when Continue is clicked.

**How to apply:** Treat these as deliberate interaction feedback, not Santa greeting playback or permission to connect mixing. Keep workshop sound controls independent of the radio and visual/reduced-motion preferences; loading, restoring choices and failed validation should stay silent.

Workshop wheels should move naturally like normal phone scrolling, not force a stop at every option.

**Why:** The owner said they were “a bit juddery right now” and asked for smoother wheels “like a normal scroll on a phone”.

**How to apply:** Preserve native touch momentum and multi-option swipes. Do not re-centre during a gesture or re-centre a wheel merely because its own settled selection came back from parent state. Keep keyboard selection, quiet restoration and deliberate click sounds intact.

The destination list should put England, Scotland, Ireland, Wales and United States first, in that exact order, then all remaining real places alphabetically. Only fantasy and space destinations belong last, alphabetically.

**Why:** The owner corrected the earlier United Kingdom request: “Ooops, default, England, Scotland, Ireland, Wales, United States, then the others”. Their later “not countries” request meant fantasy and space, not a strict sovereign-country distinction. They explicitly corrected Jersey, Guernsey, Dubai and other real destinations being mixed into that final group.

**How to apply:** Keep real territories, cities, regions and islands with real countries, even when they are not sovereign countries. This is list ordering, not permission to silently preselect an answer or relabel a different spoken recording. Keep Ireland distinct from Northern Ireland, which stays in the alphabetical remainder. Only offer a destination when its actual hosted recording is available.

Santa helper wording should not use em dashes or split words across lines.

**Why:** The owner requested “please don't use em dashes in any of the wording” and highlighted “especially” being broken into “es-” and “pecially”.

**How to apply:** Use ordinary punctuation with spaces and disable automatic hyphenation and mid-word breaking in script excerpts. Let sentences wrap between whole words so phone layouts remain readable rather than forcing the whole script onto one line.

Keep each “Santa will say…” helper card to its heading and script: no “A WORDING PREVIEW” kicker or repeated audio-unconnected disclaimer.

**Why:** The owner called “Preview wording only. Audio is not connected, so nothing is played or created.” excess words and requested its removal from the helper boxes. They also explicitly requested removing “A WORDING PREVIEW” from all Santa boxes.

**How to apply:** Keep the main design-preview notice. Do not reintroduce these extra labels or the full disclaimer in individual helper boxes merely because audio creation is still unconnected.

The workshop sounds switch belongs at the bottom-left of step one only; its chosen setting continues to apply to later stages.

**Why:** The owner requested removing “Quiet workshop feedback only; independent of Santa Radio playback” and moving the switch down, then not showing it on further pages.

**How to apply:** Keep the switch out of later stages and the review. Returning to step one can expose it again without resetting the setting. Keep workshop feedback independent of radio playback, even though the explanatory line is removed.

Keep the workshop panel in a consistent viewport position when stages change rather than repeatedly scrolling to different internal headings.

**Why:** The owner said the box was “moving about a bit too much” and asked for it to stay in the same position as each page advances.

**How to apply:** Use a stable panel anchor for Continue, Back and summary editing, accounting for the fixed navigation. Preserve keyboard focus and allow extra scrolling on short/enlarged-text layouts when needed to reveal focused controls above the radio dock. Do not freeze the panel into an inaccessible fixed-height viewport. The owner also wants this panel brought up as soon as a visitor enters the initial name field; keep focus in the field and do not re-anchor on each typed character or search-result change.
