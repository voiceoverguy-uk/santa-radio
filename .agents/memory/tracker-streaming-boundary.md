name: Tracker streaming boundary
description: Owner-approved content and privacy boundary for the YouTube map capture route.
---
Keep `/styt` as a capture of the existing live tracker map and its compact
camera/countdown toolbar, not a separate tracker or a new stream dashboard.
Exclude website chrome, radio controls, decorative effects, cookie interfaces
and automatic offers. Continue to disable optional tracking, without granting
consent or changing saved choices.

**Why:** The owner wants an uninterrupted map capture for YouTube through
Upstream, while ordinary website pages keep their usual controls and eligible
promotions. Broadcast configuration and production deployment were excluded.

**How to apply:** Share the existing actual-time and seasonal behaviour.
Changes to global overlays must preserve immediate suppression on capture entry
and restoration of the ordinary site's presentation on exit. Never treat
the absence of a consent prompt as permission.
