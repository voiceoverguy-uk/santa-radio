description: Why the adult Brevo contact step precedes the existing MP3 maker and remains separate from news consent.

The owner wants an adult's first name, surname and email submitted through the supplied Brevo form before immediate access to this website's existing recorded Santa MP3 maker. Successful submission should open the rebuilt site's maker, not an external legacy destination or an email-only access flow.

**Why:** The owner supplied the public CRM form and explicitly confirmed the post-submission destination. After reviewing an updated embed containing a required child's date of birth, they explicitly chose adult contact details only.

**How to apply:** Keep adult contact collection separate from child-name selection and on-demand recorded-audio mixing. Do not add a child's date of birth just because it appears in a supplied embed; the owner chose to remove that requirement in Brevo. Do not promise email delivery of the generated MP3 or introduce a second generator.

After successful free-maker signup on mobile, scroll to the top of the unlocked North Pole Recording Studio card immediately below the navigation, skipping the introductory text and certificate examples. Desktop retains the section-level landing position.

**Why:** The owner repeated the mobile requirement and supplied a screenshot with the recording card beneath the navigation. This supersedes the earlier mobile section-introduction destination.

**How to apply:** Apply this to the free name/email signup, including signup started on the homepage, not Studio tester-code entry. Keep the unlock announcement accessible, accommodate the mobile keyboard closing, and stop correcting when the visitor interacts. Failed signup must retain the entered details without this success scroll.

This is a signup-first, tab-session convenience flow, not authentication, email ownership verification or proof of double opt-in. The owner now requests the adult-access disclosure: “Your details are stored for Santa Radio news or updates. Read our privacy policy.”

**Why:** On 21 October 2026 the owner explicitly replaced the previous no-news-signup wording. This was a wording request, not verification or configuration of Brevo marketing enrolment or email automation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the owner-requested disclosure. Do not infer verified mailing-list behaviour or double opt-in from that text alone. Do not add API credentials or connected-service changes to replace the supplied public form without a new request. Confirm owner approval before submitting real test contacts or changing Brevo autoresponders, list settings or double-opt-in configuration.

Staff-only sign-in for the private missing-name request ledger is separate from this visitor-access flow.

**Why:** The owner approved protected staff access to requested names, without asking visitors to create accounts or attaching adult contact details to their name requests.

**How to apply:** Keep staff authentication confined to the private ledger and staff sign-in pages. Preserve the existing adult Brevo step for the public MP3 maker.

The pop-up should not include an “I am an adult” radio confirmation. Keep the unlock button directly beneath the adult email field.

**Why:** After trying the confirmation, the owner requested its removal and restoration of the unlock button’s position.

**How to apply:** Preserve the adult-contact explanation and the existing accepted-signup requirement, but do not add another confirmation or provider field.

The owner confirmed the name-collecting staff login is “great”.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved the login experience.

**How to apply:** Preserve that staff-login approach when planning related private admin features. This feedback does not independently verify all production saving or ledger operations.

Do not assume Replit-managed Clerk supports the separately Vercel-hosted website.

**Why:** On 12 October 2026, management inspection confirmed managed Clerk and official Replit documentation stated that this setup is intended for Replit-served apps, not external deployments such as Vercel. Preview login success therefore did not establish live-host compatibility.

**How to apply:** Recheck management status and current documentation when addressing live authentication. Agree a compatible authentication/hosting approach with the owner before migrating providers or moving hosting. Do not copy managed development keys as an assumed production fix.

Do not implement FTP, Mark's server integration or text-file uploads for missing-name requests. Keep the database-backed request system and protected staff ledger; staff-only TXT downloads are permitted.

**Why:** The owner explicitly cancelled the earlier FTP-copy plan and requested database-backed saving plus protected ledger downloads instead.

**How to apply:** Repair and extend the existing request storage and staff page without remote file uploads. Do not revive the withdrawn FTP approach.

The uploaded Brevo embed can differ from the current hosted form's required fields. Inspect the live form's field requirements, not just its CORS headers or JavaScript submission protocol.

**Why:** Read-only inspection found a required child date-of-birth field in the hosted form that was absent from the supplied adult-contact-only embed; mocked acceptance tests could not reveal that mismatch.

**How to apply:** Resolve extra required personal-data fields with the owner before declaring the integration complete. Do not silently collect additional child data, send invented values or bypass provider acceptance.

The free maker offers one successful message followed by a 30-minute wait. Choose randomly between the two existing outros for each eligible creation, rather than alternating them. The waiting copy rotates the owner's gift comparisons for a future more-personalised service.

**Why:** The owner revised the allowance after further research, explicitly replacing the two-message, ten-minute policy. Gift comparisons are coming-soon copy, not approval to start charging.

**How to apply:** Keep failures and cancellations free of usage charges and preserve downloads, certificates, invitation sharing and radio during the wait. Existing timed waits retain their original start when adapting to the new duration. An older single success had no creation timestamp, so it receives one persisted wait from upgrade, never a wait restarted on every refresh.

Completed free greetings should offer one compact share icon for the personalised MP3, without separate “Listen to Santa Radio” or “Share with friends” buttons.

**Why:** The owner asked to reduce clutter because the mini player already provides the radio control. This supersedes the earlier recovery-panel invitation button.

**How to apply:** Keep MP3 sharing distinct from sharing the public maker URL. Retain an accessible name and a comfortable tap target for the icon, preserve file-sharing/download fallbacks, and do not expose a greeting-share action when the recording is no longer available.
