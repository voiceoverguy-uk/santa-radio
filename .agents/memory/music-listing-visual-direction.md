name: Music listing background direction
description: Owner-requested luxury wrapping-paper treatment for the song and artist listing.
---
Use a luxury black-and-gold wrapping-paper background with little snowflakes, presents and microphones on the Music listing.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested this treatment for the artists page after approving the separate Mugshots wrapping-paper background.

**How to apply:** Scope it to the song/artist collection. Preserve catalogue artwork, search, sorting and links; do not extend the black background to individual song or artist pages without a request.
