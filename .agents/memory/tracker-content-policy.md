---
name: Tracker content boundaries
description: Keep delivery stops, holiday places, fictional antics and sourced traditions distinct.
---
Delivery stops and randomly selected holiday places serve different purposes.
Adding a delivery stop does not authorise adding a new holiday destination.

**Why:** The owner explicitly excluded new holiday destinations merely because
they become delivery stops.

**How to apply:** Keep destination-specific delivery jokes tied to the active
route stop, never to an unrelated random holiday postcard location.

Invented Santa antics must remain distinguishable from factual Christmas
information. Avoid national, religious or political stereotypes, and qualify
regional or minority traditions rather than describing them as universal.

**Why:** The owner requested family-friendly humour alongside accurate,
verifiable Christmas Around the World facts, not invented customs.

**How to apply:** Retain separate fact and make-believe labels and source new
tradition claims. Describe route coverage as represented Christmas-date UTC
offsets, never every year-round time zone.
