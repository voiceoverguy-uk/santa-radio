---
name: Homepage TV privacy exception
description: Owner-approved automatic homepage YouTube loading and the limits of that exception.
---

The owner explicitly approved loading the homepage live YouTube TV immediately
after being told this contacts YouTube before opt-in and may allow provider
cookies, analytics and advertising. Scope this exception only to the homepage
TV; do not treat it as consent for song videos, TuneIn, site analytics or Meta.
Respect refusals and withdrawal.

**Why:** The owner wants immediate homepage TV loading despite the disclosed
privacy tradeoff, not a tracking-free embed or a global permission grant.

**How to apply:** Preserve preference hydration before loading. Keep the
exception separate from stored consent, and keep homepage, cookie-settings and
privacy disclosures explicit. Expired, damaged or older records must not
silently revive media loading; only genuinely absent preferences receive the
first-visit exception. Clearing browser storage can restore that default.
