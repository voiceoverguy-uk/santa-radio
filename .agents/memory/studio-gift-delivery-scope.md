name: Studio gift delivery boundaries
description: Individual-message export permission and completed-message identity during editing.
---
The owner authorises browser-local certificate PDF, letter PDF and Santa-picture PNG delivery for the current authorised individual-message Studio flow after successful MP3 preparation. Free greetings and promotional examples remain ineligible. This permission does not activate checkout or deliver the separate multi-child family bundle.

**Why:** The owner explicitly separated individual-message gifts from live payment and family/email fulfilment.

**How to apply:** Bind gifts to the completed message, not whatever audio choices are currently being edited. Label retained older gifts clearly and switch to replacement details only on successful generation. Written-name corrections are display-only; they must not reshuffle letter wording or generate more audio. Gift retries and repeat downloads must never consume message allowance.

Names and complete letter content must stay in browser memory for export, with only generic public artwork/fonts fetched. Do not add remote export services, analytics payloads, recipient storage, or public personalised URLs.

**Why:** The owner requires local gift delivery without additional disclosure of child details.

**How to apply:** Use local file downloads, keep export failures independent from audio, and describe a download as requested rather than claiming the browser saved it.

Keep the review-stage written-name editor inside the gift-download section and update all three keepsake previews live while typing.

**Why:** The owner wants people to see the letter, certificate and picture changing as they edit the name, without changing the selected recording.

**How to apply:** Preview the local draft without saving or making requests. Preserve confirmation, cancellation and letter variation; pause gift downloads during editing so an unconfirmed draft cannot be exported.
