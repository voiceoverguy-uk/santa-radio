name: Studio gift delivery boundaries
description: Individual-message export permission and completed-message identity during editing.
---
The owner authorises browser-local certificate PDF, letter PDF and Santa-picture PNG delivery for the current authorised individual-message Studio flow after successful MP3 preparation. Free greetings and promotional examples remain ineligible. This permission does not activate checkout or deliver the separate multi-child family bundle.

**Why:** The owner explicitly separated individual-message gifts from live payment and family/email fulfilment.

**How to apply:** Review previews must immediately follow the currently selected name and choices, including after returning from editing; the owner explicitly requested this instead of retaining the first recipient's artwork. Keep export permission tied to a successful MP3 for the current choices, so changed previews unlock for download after replacement audio succeeds. Written-name corrections are display-only; they must not reshuffle letter wording or generate more audio. Gift retries and repeat downloads must never consume message allowance.

Names and complete letter content must stay in browser memory for export, with only generic public artwork/fonts fetched. Do not add remote export services, analytics payloads, recipient storage, or public personalised URLs.

**Why:** The owner requires local gift delivery without additional disclosure of child details.

**How to apply:** Use local file downloads, keep export failures independent from audio, and describe a download as requested rather than claiming the browser saved it.

Once an MP3 has succeeded, its completed gifts remain downloadable even when creating another message is blocked.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested image downloads for users who have previously generated an MP3, regardless of the Create message button's availability.

**How to apply:** Retain the completed-message reference through access-check failures and failed or pending replacements. Its downloads remain available when current choices still match. Changed review previews must not keep showing that old recipient. Do not restore history after refresh, unlock first-time gifts for unfinished recordings, or export unconfirmed spelling edits.

Starting a fresh editable selection must not discard the preceding MP3 or matching completed gifts. If its written spelling changes, those new previews need a new successful MP3 before export; the old MP3 keeps its completed name and filename.

**Why:** The local recording is the visitor's only remaining copy until downloaded; Start Again is not permission to destroy it before a replacement succeeds.

**How to apply:** Separate permission to edit a fresh selection from retention of the previous completion. Match written spelling as well as recorded choices when unlocking gift exports.

Successful generation fixtures must carry the real service's encoded selection metadata, not just MP3 bytes.

**Why:** Header-free fixtures silently use browser choices and previously hid a real mismatch between an omitted optional region and the picker's empty region.

**How to apply:** Exercise completion using echoed submitted choices, including absent regions and reordered talent properties. Keep completion tied to the successful request rather than later review state.

Keep the pre-completion written-name editor inside the gift-download section and update all three keepsake previews live while typing. Once the current message's MP3 succeeds, remove the pencil and any open editor, discard unconfirmed drafts and retain the confirmed spelling as read-only.

**Why:** The owner wants live previews before generation; their latest instruction supersedes earlier permission to correct written spelling after completion.

**How to apply:** Preview the local draft without saving or making requests. Preserve confirmation, cancellation and letter variation before success. Completion must survive preparation, export activity and access errors; restore editing only for an explicit fresh message, never merely because downloads are disabled.

Do not allow swear words in written-name spellings or generated keepsake previews.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested disabling “Use this spelling” for swear words, including profanity within a longer entry, and explaining that bad language prevents image creation.

**How to apply:** Explain the restriction beside the field and in unavailable-download guidance, keep offensive drafts out of artwork, and preserve the last valid spelling. Check word forms and common disguises without treating innocent substrings in real names as profanity.
