The owner clarified that the gameplay recording belongs in the HOME PAGE Santa
Dash — Back to the Grotto banner. Adapting that banner to include a dark landscape
phone is approved; the previously existing handset was on the Apps page, which
must remain separate from this homepage change.

**Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the assumed handset location and approved
the homepage adaptation while retaining the rooftop artwork, heading and store
links.

**How to apply:** Preserve the full game frame rather than cropping important
gameplay to fit a decorative phone. Keep the store destinations clear and
playback controls separate. This is silent promotional gameplay with conservative
visibility-based autoplay and still/reduced-motion defaults, not an embedded
playable game or permission to redesign the Apps page.

The owner wants a bottom-left inset approximately TWO THIRDS of the original
large phone width, or twice the first one-third-width trial. Keep the entire
handset proportionate with the same neat inset, inside the original artwork
banner on mobile as well, and keep promotional text and the badge unobscured.

**Why:** The initial phone obscured the artwork, but the subsequent one-third
trial was too small. The owner requested the intermediate size.

**How to apply:** Preserve the inset treatment when changing this banner.
Constrain mobile width as needed to avoid obscuring promotional text; do not
move the handset below the artwork without a new request.

The homepage artwork must stay on the original “Enjoy Santa Radio while you
play” image, without rotating or dissolving, including during gameplay.

**Why:** The owner approved this single artwork against their layout reference;
the reference is not a flattened replacement for the real video.

**How to apply:** Keep the live gameplay layered over the original artwork.
Do not apply this single-image rule to the separate Apps promotion.

The whole handset, including the frame and gameplay, should be 90% opaque
(10% transparent); surrounding artwork, heading, links and playback controls
must remain fully opaque.

**Why:** The owner requested a return to the previous 10% transparency after
reviewing the more transparent version, without changing size, position or playback.

**How to apply:** Apply opacity to the handset group only, never the banner or
individual nested pieces (which would compound the transparency).

The owner also wants Classic running Santa beneath the homepage Santa Dash
banner, travelling one way and then back again, at the very bottom edge of
that section rather than above its bottom padding.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested this addition, then clarified with a
screenshot that Santa should run on the section's bottom edge.

**How to apply:** Keep the runner beneath, not over the artwork or gameplay.
Keep his feet at the section floor and the download button just above his head;
the owner requested moving the heading, banner and button down together to close
that gap. Preserve the existing banner, handset and download link, and respect
reduced motion.

Occasional random jumps using the owner-supplied Classic jump artwork are approved
for the homepage runner only. Keep the approved floor and content placement.

**Why:** After supplying and discussing the jump add-on, the owner explicitly
asked to add the jumps randomly.

**How to apply:** Keep jumps small and infrequent, respect reduced motion and
visibility, and leave the Studio preparation runner independent.
