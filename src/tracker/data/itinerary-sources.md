# Estimated 290-destination itinerary

The owner's supplied list contains 290 actual places after section headings are
excluded (the pasted numbering has repeats). Every place is retained, including
the three Antarctic research stations. This list does not include American Samoa;
Honolulu is the final destination in Christmas-time UTC order.

## Geographic data

City coordinates, IANA time-zone identifiers and stable feature IDs come from
[GeoNames cities15000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/), downloaded
8 November 2026. GeoNames data is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The bundled JSON is a selected, name-normalised derivative; no live geocoder or
tracking service is contacted at runtime.

Palikir and Queenstown were added explicitly because their population sizes
exclude them from cities15000. Kiritimati is represented by its inhabited island
location rather than a regional centroid. Antarctic stations use representative
station coordinates: McMurdo (-77.8463,166.6682), Amundsen–Scott (-89.999,0;
near-pole display convention), and Rothera (-67.5684,-68.125).
See the [COMNAP station catalogue](https://www.comnap.aq/antarctic-facilities-information/)
and GeoNames feature IDs retained in the JSON.
McMurdo and the South Pole use New Zealand station clocks; Rothera uses UTC−3.
Coordinates are for map presentation, not precision navigation.

## Timing and fiction

Local clocks use the browser/Node IANA rules for Christmas Day in the selected
year, including daylight saving and fractional offsets. Each time-zone band
begins at local midnight; its stops are geographically ordered and assigned
distinct slots before the next band. London anchors the UTC-zero band.
Each slot is part dwelling and part travel, so every stop becomes active.
The existing 10:00 UTC 24 December–11:20 UTC 25 December window is retained.

The ordering, stop durations, estimated gifts and travel are fictional; time-zone
information is not evidence of Santa's location. This is not NORAD data or
verified live tracking. Santa antics are explicitly fictional and make no
claims about national, religious or political customs.
