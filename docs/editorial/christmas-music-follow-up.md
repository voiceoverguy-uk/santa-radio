## Frankie Goes to Hollywood — The Power of Love (388)

The user confirmed this is the intended Holly Johnson entry and requested reuse of the Holly Johnson portrait.

**Before:** Frankie Goes to Hollywood, formed in 1980, were a British band popular in the mid-1980s. The group was fronted by Holly Johnson, with Paul Rutherford, Peter Gill, Mark O'Toole, and Brian Nash

**After:** Fronted by Holly Johnson, Liverpool’s Frankie Goes to Hollywood made a striking impression with “Relax” and “Two Tribes”. “The Power of Love” reveals a gentler side, with Johnson’s expressive voice leading a sweeping ballad. Its Nativity-themed video helped give the song a lasting Christmas connection — a quieter moment from a band rarely known for understatement.

## Frank Sinatra and Dean Martin — Auld Lang Syne (387)

The user requested a brief biography of both artists rather than recording-specific claims. Attribution of the embedded performance remains unverified.

**Before:** The Rat Pack was a supergroup of actors originally centered on their leader, Humphrey Bogart, featuring Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin, Sammy Davis, Jr., Peter Lawford and Joey Bishop

**After:** Frank Sinatra’s expressive voice made “My Way” and “Fly Me to the Moon” enduring favourites. Dean Martin brought an easy charm to “That’s Amore” and “Everybody Loves Somebody”. Friends and fellow Rat Pack entertainers, both combined singing with film and television careers — a generous helping of old-school showmanship.
