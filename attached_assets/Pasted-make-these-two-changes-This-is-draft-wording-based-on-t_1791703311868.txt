Christmas Radio iPhone and iPad app
Using the app

You can listen to Christmas Radio without creating an account. The native app does not ask you to provide your name, email address or other contact details.

The app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. It does not access your advertising identifier, microphone, camera, contacts, photo library or device location. Background playback, AirPlay and CarPlay are audio-playback features and do not involve microphone recording.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers your Snowfall, Blizzard and Festive lights preferences on your device. It also stores copies of artist artwork and recent song information to reduce downloads and display previously available information when a connection is unavailable.

The app does not upload these saved preferences or cached copies to us. iOS may remove cached files automatically. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data from that installation. Device settings may also be included in backups managed by Apple; deleting the app does not necessarily remove existing backups.

Connections needed for radio playback

The app connects over HTTPS to:

Citrus3, at global.citrus3.com, to receive the live radio stream.
Santa Radio, at www.santaradio.co.uk, to obtain current and upcoming song information and artist artwork.
These connections necessarily disclose your IP address and normal technical request information to the relevant servers. This can include request times, requested resources and information supplied by the networking software. Requests may indicate when you connect to the stream or request song information and artwork.

The app does not create an account-based personal listening-history profile. However, the relevant servers or hosting providers may maintain connection logs or listener statistics.
